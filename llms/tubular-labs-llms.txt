# Tubular Labs

> Tubular Labs is a social video intelligence and measurement company. Its platform indexes cross-platform creator, video, and audience data across YouTube, Instagram, Facebook, Twitch, and other social video platforms, and the Tubular API offers programmatic access to that data for content strategy, creator discovery, competitive tracking, and audience insight.

## APIs

- [Tubular API](https://tubularlabs.com/resources/tubular-api/): Programmatic access to cross-platform creator, video, and audience data for brand-mention monitoring, competitor tracking, creator discovery, and trend/category analysis.
