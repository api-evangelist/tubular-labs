When marketers ask, “How do I identify the best partnerships to reach our brand goals?” the answer often comes down to one thing: data-driven audience insights. Too often, partnerships are chosen based on surface-level metrics like views and engagement. But with social video intelligence, brands can uncover deeper audience interests to discover unexpected collaboration opportunities.
